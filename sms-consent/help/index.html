Saifely Emergency Alerts: SMS Help

Saifely sends SMS only to people who have been listed as an emergency contact by a cyclist using the Saifely crash-detection app, and only when that rider's device detects a possible crash.

How it works

  • The rider lists your phone number as an emergency contact on their cycling computer.
  • You receive a one-time invitation SMS asking you to confirm or opt out.
  • If you confirm, you'll only receive a text if their device detects a possible crash.
  • Message frequency is event-based, typically less than one per month per rider.

Keywords

  • YES or START: confirm you'd like to receive alerts.
  • STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT: stop receiving SMS from Saifely.
  • HELP or INFO: get this help reply.

Costs

Saifely doesn't charge you. Standard message and data rates may apply from your carrier.
