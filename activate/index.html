It Takes Some Convincing

Four checks run in sequence before anything leaves your bike. A pothole has to pass all of them, and potholes are bad at that.

  1. 2.5G

    A real impact

    Riding sits near 1G. Hard enough that a rough surface almost never reaches it.

  2. 45°

    The bike goes down

    Both how far the Karoo is tilted and how much it turned since impact, because a gravel skid and a corner crash show up differently.

  3. 15s

    You stop moving

    Speed, rotation and pedal power. Stand up and lift the bike and it stands down.

  4. 30s

    You do not answer

    On screen for you, or a passer by, to cancel. Only then does anyone get a message.

We would rather text your partner about a pothole than say nothing about a crash.
