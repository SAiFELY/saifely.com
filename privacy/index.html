Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

Device data: Karoo serial number, hardware type, and app version during device registration.

Emergency contacts: Names, phone numbers, and email addresses you provide for crash notification.

Crash event data: Sensor readings (accelerometer, gyroscope), GPS location, and timestamps when a crash event is detected.

Payment data: Processed by Stripe. We receive your email address and payment confirmation but never your card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide the crash detection and emergency notification service. Specifically:

  • To send emergency alerts (SMS and email) to your designated contacts
  • To share your GPS location with emergency contacts during a crash event
  • To manage your license and device registration
  • To improve crash detection accuracy through anonymized sensor data analysis

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. Sensitive fields (contact information, GPS coordinates) are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. API keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes.

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

  • Twilio: to deliver SMS alerts to your emergency contacts
  • SendGrid: to deliver email alerts to your emergency contacts
  • Stripe: to process payments

We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Crash event data is retained for 12 months. Account data is retained while your license is active and for 30 days after expiry. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@saifely.com to exercise these rights.

7. SMS Program (A2P 10DLC)

Program name: Saifely Emergency Alerts.Purpose: Saifely sends SMS only to people who have been listed as an emergency contact by a Saifely subscriber, and only when that rider's device detects a possible crash, plus an initial one-time invitation when the recipient is first added.

Message types: opt-in invitation, crash alert, "I'm OK" cancellation, and replies to the keywords below.Message frequency: event-based, typically less than one per month per rider.Cost: Saifely does not charge recipients for messages. Standard message and data rates may apply from the recipient's carrier.

Consent: recipients receive a one-time invitation SMS and must reply YES (or use the confirmation link) before they will receive crash alerts. The link is also delivered via WhatsApp and email when those channels are configured. Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT, or by using the opt-out link at /sms-consent/help.Help: reply HELP to any message, see /sms-consent/help, or email support@saifely.com.Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at support@saifely.com.
